# 1008 Interim dormancy (P6 step 1): residents and animals outside the integrated window ([GAP] and as-built, harness plane, 2026-10-09)

Author: 8d998a ("1008latenight"). Status: design, then code behind `EDEN_WINDOW_DORMANCY` (default off; requires `EDEN_WINDOW`). Reviewer: c3. Implements c3's order of 2026-10-09 "residents left outside the window keep running anchor movement through void (no walls) and needs".

## 1. What happens today (measured on the real server, `tools/live_smoke_window.py` phase_walk)

The window follows the player (`815daed`). Every resident left behind keeps its row, so it is still in `h_mask`: needs tick, escalations and the model plane keep acting, and an anchor-driven resident keeps moving through terrain that has no rows, no walls and no void boxes (void boxes sit only along the edges of integrated chunks). After a 1 200 unit walk, 5 of 5 space-0 residents were more than 5 chunks from the player; after 3 400 units, 7. In a long absence they would wander off the map, starve or die unseen.

## 2. Predicate (the one definition everybody uses)

A human row is **dormant** when: it is a live human, `H_SPACE == 0`, and the chunk containing its window-local position is **not in `WindowManager.chunks`** (not integrated). (Rows in interiors are never dormant: pockets are always loaded.) Animals: the same test on `M_TYPE in ANIMAL_TYPES`, `M_SPACE == 0` (no animal rows exist yet; the code is there so P5 step c2 inherits it).

* `engine._dormant_h`: `BoolTensor[MAX_HUMANS]`, `engine._dormant_a`: `BoolTensor[MAX_ENTITIES]`, refreshed once at the start of `step()` by `harness.dormancy.refresh(engine)` (vectorised: floor of the positions, a membership test against the integrated set; microseconds). All False with the flag off.
* `harness.dormancy.is_dormant(engine, slot)` for scalar callers.
* One-tick lag at the frontier is harmless: a resident cannot walk into a non-integrated chunk (void boxes), so a row is in a non-integrated chunk only because the window moved away from it or the row was placed there.
* **Hysteresis is the keep ring, not extra code**: a chunk is integrated from the 5x5 simulate ring and kept in the 7x7 ring, so a resident at the edge of the player's surroundings does not flicker.

## 3. Freeze: three layers, so no system has to know

1. **Masks.** `engine.step()` builds the registry context `ctx["h_mask"]` and `ctx["a_mask"]` without dormant rows, and ORs them back after the registry loop (the sync, id and death detection must keep seeing them as alive: `_prev_h_mask & ~h_mask` would otherwise report them dead). Needs, hunger and thirst, infection, mood, social, nature on humans, the instinct movement: all skip them.
2. **Sweeps.** `HumanRuntime.process_escalations` (and the talk sweep) excludes dormant slots, so no model call, no escalation and no decision is requested for them.
3. **Net.** At the start of `step()` the dormant rows are copied (a handful of rows), and at the very end they are written back: position, needs, hp, every column bit for bit. This catches whatever the model plane or another hook does to a dormant row (an anchor mover step, a god command) and gives a testable invariant: **a dormant row is bit-identical from tick to tick**. It does not undo records or the ledger; for those the model plane skips dormant residents (section 5).

## 4. Wake (the chunk is integrated again) and the catch-up rule

On the first tick a row is not dormant: `elapsed = tick - dormant_since`. Recommendation: **pause while away, closed-form on return, capped so nobody dies of the absence**:

* hunger: `min(hunger + HUNGER_TICK_RATE * rate("needs") * elapsed, 80)`; thirst: the same with a cap of 75. Both sit under the damage thresholds (90 / 100). A resident who was already at or over the threshold when frozen stays there and dies by the normal rule right after waking (the rule "really kills them").
* age: exact, `H_AGE += age_step() * elapsed` (a linear rate; old-age death is then the normal check).
* stamina set to its full value, threat and mood moved to their baselines (a rested resident), hp unchanged, infection unchanged.
* resident record: no change (memory stays, plans are dropped by the driver on resume, see 5).
* The rule is data in `harness/dormancy.py` (`CATCH_UP`), applied in one function, so tuning is one place. With `EDEN_CALENDAR` off the rates are the legacy ones, as everywhere.

## 5. For the model plane (latenight2): the exact predicate

`harness.dormancy.is_dormant(engine, slot)` (or `engine._dormant_h[slot]`). While true the driver must not: step the anchor mover, request a decision, start or advance a ladder errand, apply an action. On the tick it turns false the driver treats the resident as newly arrived: re-plan from its actual position (the stale plan is dropped, `rebase_model_plane` already shows which holders keep positions). `systems/actions/*` stays latenight2's; this document only gives the predicate and the wake event (`engine._dormancy_log`: `(tick, "sleep" | "wake", uid)`).

## 6. Cost and tests

Cost: one vectorised refresh per tick, a copy of the dormant rows, two mask ANDs. Measured with 5 to 7 dormant residents: reported with the code (expect below 0.1 ms; the saving is that dormant residents no longer run the movement and escalation work).

Tests: (1) a dormant row is bit-identical over 200 ticks with the registry running; (2) it is not reported dead or despawned in the frame/events; (3) wake applies the cap numbers exactly and never kills; (4) flag off: strict goldens unchanged, `_dormant_h` all False; (5) the window follow test, then walk away and back: the same residents, uids and slots, positions equal; (6) a save while residents are dormant restores them dormant (the predicate is derived from positions and the integrated set, nothing new is saved except `dormant_since`, which goes into the window section as `dormant_since: {uid: tick}`, additive to format 3); (7) live smoke: the residents left behind do not move (max 0.0 units) while the player is away.

## 7. Not in this step

Coarse background simulation of dormant residents (a village that lives on while the player is away): that is the full P6. Built since as a catch-up applied at wake (R6.5, `EDEN_OFFSCREEN`): section 9. Dormant structures: `./structure_store_gap.md` (next). A resident whose home or work anchor is a dormant structure: the anchor is a uid and a position; while dormant nothing reads it (test 7 of the store design covers the resume).

## 8. As built (ffeeced, b13eb77) and what the live runs changed

* Flag `EDEN_WINDOW_DORMANCY=1`; `engine_hooks.install` refuses to boot without `EDEN_WINDOW=1` (`SpaceConfigError` naming the flag).
* **Grace period** `GRACE_TICKS = 40`: a row must be outside the integrated chunks for 40 ticks before it goes dormant (a fresh world integrates one chunk per tick; without it the first ticks froze the player's own human).
* **A player's body is never dormant**: `WindowManager.controlled_slots()` = humans with a session plus the avatars a controller incarnated (`engine._incarnated_human_by_controller`). The window follows the same set, so a session that lapses after a few idle seconds does not send the window back to the village under a parked avatar (found live: origin snapped back, the avatar was left behind).
* **`thaw` follows a re-base**: the window hook re-bases in the middle of a step; writing the saved old-frame row back made the player look far from the new origin, the window re-based again every tick (runaway, found live, epoch +1 per tick). `freeze` records the origin and `thaw` shifts the saved rows by the difference.
* Catch-up applied as designed (hunger cap 80, thirst cap 75, age exact, stamina full, threat 0; mood and hp untouched).
* Model plane: `actions/dormancy_gate.py` (3543ada) skips dormant residents in the agent driver and the other sweeps; the legacy anchor driver skips them in `systems/roster/driver.anchor_driven_slots` (b13eb77).
* Measured live (tools/live_smoke_window.py, 47 checks): 7 residents outside the frame moved 0.000 units over 6 s while the player stood away; tick pace unchanged (112 to 119 ticks per 6 s at 20 Hz); `/status` work_ms p50 15.5 ms with the flag versus 16.9 ms without, p99 34.2 versus 33.9 (same walk, machine shared with other runs: no measurable cost).
* `dormant_since` is saved in the window section (key present only while something is dormant, so every other format 3 save is unchanged; the validator accepts it as optional). Not done: animals are masked and frozen by the same code but no animal rows exist in the window yet.
* **Animals, as built since (R6.6, verified 2026-11-19):** animal rows in the window now exist, made by the creature store (`harness/creature_store.py`, `EDEN_CREATURES`): every species of `systems/creatures/table.py`, the legacy ids 40 to 44 included. With both flags a creature outside every integrated chunk is dormant at once (animals have no grace period) and frozen bit for bit until the creature store's wanderer sweep (every 20 ticks) stores it with the chunk it stands in; `thaw` does not write it back into the released row (uid and `M_TYPE` guard). The wake catch-up is for humans only: a stored creature gets the store's own closed-form catch-up when its chunk is integrated again. Tests: `tests/test_r66_animals_dormancy.py` (8).

## 9. As built since: offscreen villages (R6.5, 2026-11-19, uncommitted)

Option A of the R6.5 plan (`.orchestration/wsim/handovers/1119wsimimplementation-plan-R6.5.md`, births addendum `...-R6.5-births.md`): a lazy catch-up when a dormant resident wakes, in place of section 4's capped rule. Rulings: D1 death while away by the normal rules; D2 option A now, a live background tick (option B) later; D3 births in this step, counted in closed form and placed at wake next to the mother; D4 a group is the home anchor plus the connected dormant chunks.

* Flag `EDEN_OFFSCREEN=1` (default off), needs `EDEN_WINDOW_DORMANCY=1`: `engine_hooks.install` refuses to boot without it (`SpaceConfigError` naming both flags). Flag off, the wake is section 4's capped catch-up, unchanged.
* **Where:** `harness/offscreen.py`; `dormancy.refresh` hands every row that stopped being dormant this tick to `offscreen.wake_all` instead of `wake`, and calls `offscreen.retry` while births wait to be placed.
* **Groups (D4):** the woken rows and the rows still dormant are grouped by chunk (a resident's own chunk and its home anchor's chunk; chunks touching, Chebyshev distance 1, join a group). The whole group is advanced once, from its earliest `dormant_since`; members still dormant get their result cached and written when they wake, so a group is never advanced twice.
* **The coarse model** (`coarse_ticks`, a pure function of plain data; `coarse_days` is the same in days), per step: food rows in the group's chunks and one chunk around them regrow in closed form under their cap (`M_GROWTH_RATE`, `M_NODE_CAP`, resource nodes only); hunger and thirst rise at the normal rate; a resident whose hunger would pass the fed set point (30) eats back down to it, so the food a group needs is what brings each member back to the set point, and the fed fraction is the food available over that (the unfed share keeps its rise); thirst stops at its set point (25), water being taken as available; a need below its set point is never raised to it (R6.5-fix, after the calibration run); starvation and dehydration damage count the ticks at or over 90; healing while hunger is under 70; age by the calendar's age step; mating cooldown decays; death by hp 0 (starvation, dehydration) or age over life expectancy (old age). After it, stamina is full and threat is 0, as in section 4. Positions never change (the model plane treats a woken resident as newly arrived, section 5).
* **Roster residents (R6.5-roster):** a resident with a roster binding never eats from food rows: the roster needs loop (`systems/roster/needs.py`) sends it to the village site at the threshold (30; the starter village: `village:use_campfire`, `village:use_well`), and the need goes back to 0 (a ration of 25 on the spot when it has no site). Away, the loop is applied in closed form per need (`_loop`): under the threshold the need is what it rose to; every crossing drops it back (site: to 0; ration: by 25). Sites are unlimited: no food row is drained and a roster resident never starves while away. **Approximation:** the walk to the site and the stand there (40 ticks water, 60 food) are taken as instant, so each trip is off by at most those ticks of rise (on the starter village, needs scale 0.015: under 0.05 hunger per trip, a trip every ~17 legacy days). With the agent ladder and the market balance on (`EDEN_AGENT_LADDER`, the agent driver), food waits for the ladder's limit (90; the ladder's own feeding below it is not modelled), and with `EDEN_LADDER_STARVE=1` food falls back to the fed/unfed model. Non-roster residents in the same group keep the fed/unfed model.
* **Births (D3):** each game day a ready mother (the normal rule `_slot_ready_for_mating`: age 18 or more, cooldown 0, hunger and thirst 55 or less, hp 45 or more; and a ready male in the same group) conceives with chance `BIRTH_P_PER_READY_DAY` (0.05, **uncalibrated placeholder**), drawn from the counter stream `(world seed, day, "offscreen.birth", mother uid)`, so the result does not depend on the order of the residents. Her cooldown becomes 1.0 (one life-year). The child lives the rest of the away time by the same model (a child that dies while away is logged and never placed). **A roster world has no births at all** (the normal rule, `population_fixed`): the starter village gets 0 births whatever the chance is.
* **Placing a birth at wake:** the uid is reserved at once (`_make_human_id`); the child goes into the first free human row, at a standable point (`spatial.standable_spawn_point`) within 4 units of the mother (the father, else the group's anchor), in an integrated chunk; `instinct_born` with the age it reached, then the normal id sync emits `human_born` and the parent links. With no free row (`no_slot`, `MAX_HUMANS` 100) or no standable point (`no_place`) the birth waits in `engine._offscreen_pending` with its reason and is retried every 20 ticks; the delay counts towards its age. Counters `births`, `births_placed`, `births_deferred`.
* **Save:** the window section gets the optional key `offscreen` (`pending_births`, `counters`) only while a birth waits, so every other save is byte-identical with the flag on or off. Loading a save with that key into a session without `EDEN_OFFSCREEN` is refused (`SaveFormatError`, naming the flag; the save is not changed), as the calendar refusals.
* **Not calibrated:** the set points of `CATCH_UP_V2` (hunger 30, thirst 25) and `BIRTH_P_PER_READY_DAY` are placeholders, marked `calibrated: False`. `tools/offscreen_equivalence.py` (an active headless village sampled against `coarse_ticks`) measures them. Runs on 2026-11-19, 5 legacy days each, seed 1 (`reports/r65-verify-1119.md`): the roster village (8 residents, 0 food rows) never reaches a steady state, since needs rise about 9 in 5 days against a threshold of 30; without the roster the village (4 residents) dies of thirst within 1400 ticks with 0 births (0.65 ready mother-days), so p is not measurable. The placeholders stay and stay uncalibrated. They do not apply to roster residents (the needs loop decides those). Equivalence check with `--start-hungry` (needs 60 after warm-up): each resident walks once to the campfire and the well and is back near 0 by tick 300; at tick 12000 live against coarse is hunger 8.82 to 8.88 against 9.00, thirst 12.46 to 12.55 against 12.60, population 8 / 8, deaths 0 / 0. The gap (0.12 to 0.18 hunger) is the 160 to 250 ticks of the one trip, the walk and stand approximation above.
* Client contract: no wire change (`design_docs/contracts/client_contract.md`, the dormancy line).
* Tests: `tests/test_r65_offscreen.py` (38): the pure model (capped needs, the roster loop against a tick-by-tick reference, the ladder limit), the engine wake, births (pure and engine: placement, deferral and retry, no standable point, flag off), the save key and its refusal, and the boot refusal.
