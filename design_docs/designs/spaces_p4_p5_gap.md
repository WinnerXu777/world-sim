## 1. Corrections to the earlier design (found while preparing this)
1. **Hook placement.** `DISPATCH.run_step_hooks` runs in `Engine.step` after the registry loop and after the movement reconcile (`map_test.py` 4935), before `_sync_slot_ids_and_events` and before the frame is built. It is not an `_registry` entry, so "priority 81, before regrow 82" in design 4.7 is void. The window hook is a step hook: integration, unload and re-base happen at the end of tick t, rows are exported in the same frame, and they take part in the systems from tick t+1. **read.**
2. **Interior pockets move.** Design section 5 of `./spaces_p3_gap.md` proposed a pocket pool at `[4096, 8192)`. float32 ulp there is 4.9e-4, four times the 1.2e-4 worst case Stage 1 already has (1024 to 2048) and above `EMBED_TOL = 1e-4`. Revised pool in section 5.4: |coordinate| below 2048, outside the window-local range in x.
3. **`register_entity` is too slow for window integration, measured.** One `map_geo.register_entity` costs 411 to 441 us whatever the radius (full-frame 150 x 112 mask; radii 6, 30 and 80 tried). A chunk has 5.1 feature entities on average (max 15): 2.2 ms mean and 6.6 ms worst case per chunk for geometry alone, against the integration target of 2 ms per tick for the whole step. Sparse bounding-box masks (P4) are therefore a precondition of P5, not an optimisation.

## 2. Thread and ownership (from 2f0fd3, relayed; **read** by them)
`Engine.step` runs on the event-loop thread (`await self.scheduler.run(self.step, ...)`, `FixedStepScheduler` has no thread or executor). The routes share that thread, so no mailbox is needed. `ChunkService` is not thread-safe but its process pool is internal: workers return bytes, collected in `pump()`. `step()` is synchronous, so a `pump()` inside the tick blocks the loop for its duration; `inline_budget = 1` chunk. Consequences for the window:
- the integrate hook calls `request / peek / get / pump / pending_count` directly, at most one `pump()` per tick;
- **one service instance per process.** Today `api/chunk_routes.default_service()` creates its own service and process pool lazily. With the window there would be two pools (the routes' and the window's), generating the same chunks twice. Proposal: the window manager owns the instance and `build_chunk_router(get_engine, service_factory)` receives it (`service_factory` already exists). Needs 2f0fd3's agreement on who closes it (`close()` at shutdown) and on `inline_budget` in the tick.

## 4. P4: `map_geo` window mode (no window yet)
**Goal.** `map_geo` can run on a configurable tile grid and keep sparse masks, with the default path untouched. P4 ships no flag of its own: window mode is entered only by calling `map_geo.configure_window(...)`, which nothing calls until P5.
**Changes.**
- `map_geo.configure_window(grid_w, grid_h, origin_tile)` and a reset; `_px_to_tile_idx` reads module variables instead of the imported `GRID_W/H` (defaults equal today's 150 x 112); `TILE_TO_ENTITY` keys stay flat indices of the configured grid.
- Masks stored as `(row0, col0, small bool tensor)` over the shape's bounding box, built from the shape only (no full-frame meshgrid); `MAP_GEO[eid]["tile_mask"]` keeps working for legacy readers through a lazily expanded property or a helper `tiles_of(eid)`; readers of `tile_mask` found so far: `nature/hazards.py` (fire damage), `nature/disasters.py` (flood damage), `world_snapshot` geometry, `get_geo_json`. **not verified:** other readers (first task: grep).
- `harness/spaces.clip_tile_mask` (P3) assumes a full-frame mask; it is adapted to clip the bounding box.
- Save: geometry without masks, rebuilt from shapes on restore. This changes the pickled payload, so it is only done under the window format (section 5.6); the format-1 and -2 geometry stays as it is.
- Space-aware tile lookups (needed by pockets, section 5.4): `entity_at(ax, ay)` finds the frame of the point (`point_space`) and indexes that frame.
**Measured sizes (seed 1008, 100 chunks, 590 feature entities).** Radius mean 24.8 units, p50 16, p95 69, max 96 (forest 72, ore vein 96). Bounding box at 8 units per tile: mean 92 tiles, p95 371, max 676, against 50 176 tiles for a 7 x 7 chunk window grid (224 x 224): 0.2 percent mean. 400 entities as full masks would be 20 MB; sparse about 37 000 booleans.
**Contract cost (measured, same run; box counts include the 69 of the village contract).** `world_contract(engine)`: 311 us at 69 boxes, 615 us at 184, 960 us at 297, 1476 us at 484 (2.8 us per added box; it is called once per tick plus by the 14 other sites). `slide` per actor call: 16 us, 38, 50, 76 us. At 100 residents and 484 boxes that is 7.6 ms per tick in `slide` alone (**inference**: 100 is the cap, not today's 8). Broadphase (give each actor the boxes of its chunk and the 8 neighbours) is therefore needed before 100 residents walk in a full window; with 8 residents it is 0.6 ms and can wait. The revision hash is part of the 311 us; caching it until the integrated set changes is the cheap first step.
**Path planning (measured, weak):** `plan_path` over a 1792 x 1792 unit area took 144 to 210 ms per request in the first two runs; the later runs read 0.2 ms, which I take for a cached or failed search, not a result (my script did not check the path). **not verified.** Whatever the real number is, a long request is two orders above a tick; window mode caps request bounds (P7 does proper waypoints); P5 only needs that residents inside the simulation ring can path within the integrated area.
**Proof.**
1. `tools/golden_trace.py --sparse-geo` (calls `configure_window` with the full finite frame before boot): strict check against the default golden, `--resources`, and `--spaces`: **bit-identical** (same hashes). This is stronger than a new golden.
2. Unit tests: sparse mask equals the full mask on its box and is empty outside, for random circles and polygons (including shapes clipped by the frame edge); `entities_in_radius`, `entity_at`, fire/flood damage unchanged on the P3 leak fixtures; snapshot round trip.
3. Measurement gate under a MEASURE lock: `register_entity` per entity below 50 us (target; currently 411 to 441), `world_contract` and `slide` as above, reported before and after the revision cache.
**Legacy files:** `map_geo.py` (about 80 lines), `harness/spaces.py` (mine), `world_snapshot.py` geometry section under the window format only (about 20 lines), `tools/golden_trace.py` (mine). Flag-off effect: none (default path not entered).

## 5. P5: `EDEN_WINDOW`
### 5.1 Flags
`EDEN_WINDOW=1` requires `EDEN_SPACES=1`, which requires `EDEN_PERCEPTION_BOUNDED=1`; the boot check (`spaces.check_flags`) names the first missing flag. `EDEN_CHUNK_ROUTES` stays independent but, when both are on, the routes use the window's service (section 2). Defaults off: with the flag off nothing below is entered and the goldens stay bit-identical.

### 5.2 Components (all new files unless marked)
- `harness/window.py`: `WindowManager` (state: origin chunk, epoch, integrated set, per-chunk row lists, per-chunk wall boxes, integration log, row budget), `SlotAllocator`, `release_slots(engine, slots)`. The sequencing (`rings.plan_stream`, `blocked_by_void`) is 2f0fd3's and is used as is.
- Step hook `window_integrate` (section 1.1): plan on change or every 4 ticks (planning each tick p99 3.1 ms, measured by 2f0fd3), `pump()` once, integrate at most one chunk or unload at most one, re-base if due. One chunk's work: rows in one vectorised write (ids `c<cx>_<cy>:<kind><n>` and `M_SPACE = 0`), geometry (sparse, P4), wall boxes into the per-chunk list, index update, event `window_integrate` in the ledger. Replay uses the log, not the service (design 4.7).
- Void boxes: one box per integrated-chunk edge whose neighbour is not integrated (design 4.5), recomputed when the set changes.
- `systems/movement/spatial.py` (legacy, about 20 lines): `_compose` appends `engine._window_boxes` (chunk boxes + void boxes) when present; `world_contract` revision is composed from `(static hash, window set hash)` and cached.
- `map_test.py` (legacy, about 30 lines): hook install; `/bootstrap` `world` block and `window_origin` / `window_epoch` in `/frame`, `/map_delta`, `/bootstrap`, `/human_view/window` payloads; client contract section 4 stays the wire spec.
- Residents: `runtime/resident_store.py` `remember_resource` stores `uid` and an `AbsPos`; consumers per the table in section 3 (rows 1 to 3).
- Re-base (`WindowManager.rebase`): shifts by whole multiples of 256 the rows with `M_SPACE == 0` / `H_SPACE == 0` in both tensors, the `MAP_GEO` shapes and tile origin of space 0, `_actor_prev_positions`, the **registered primary map contract** (its `bounds`, `boxes`, `anchors`, `entities`, `portals` anchors are stored in layout coordinates, `map_contract.py`; the roster reads anchors from it every tick), roster `final_positions` / `checkpoint` / mover plans (Model-plane file; needs a re-base callback they implement), resident memory positions, `explore_toward` `target_place`, pathing plan caches (invalidated by the revision). Pocket rows are not shifted. List to be turned into a test that scans `vars(engine)` and the tensors for position-bearing state.

### 5.3 Flow constants (unchanged from the design)
`REBASE_AT = 2` chunks, local range `[-1280, 1536)`, `WINDOW_ROW_BUDGET = 800`, rings 5 x 5 simulate / 7 x 7 keep, one chunk per tick.

### 5.4 Interior pocket pool (revised)
Interiors cannot live in the overworld frame once it slides, and they cannot sit near the origin because the window-local range `[-1280, 1536)` covers every coordinate the authored interiors use today (x 620 to 1056). Pool: two vertical strips outside that range in x, inside |coordinate| < 2048 so the ulp stays at most 1.2e-4:
- strip A: x in `[1792, 2048)`, strip B: x in `[-2048, -1792)`, y in `[-2048, 2048)`; pockets of 256 x 256 units (32 x 32 tiles), 16 per strip, 32 in all. The authored interiors are 96 x 80 units (smith, lodge, west cottage); a pocket holds up to 32 x 32 tiles.
- A space's pocket origin becomes **saved state** (`_spaces`, in the window format) instead of the `EDEN_MAP_ORIGINS` constant: the three authored interiors get pockets 0, 1, 2 when the world is created and keep them. `register_world_map(origin=...)` already accepts the origin; the loader reads the pocket instead of the env variable in window mode. Dynamic activation (assign on entry, release when empty) is deferred to P6 with dormancy.
- Distance isolation stops being a rule: the P2/P3 space filters (masks, `point_space`, clipped tiles) are the guarantee, and the leak test is re-run in window mode.
- Tile lookups per space (section 4): each pocket is its own 32 x 32 tile frame.
- **As built (a66ea86, c3 decision): `POCKET_STEP = 1`, the authored interiors take pockets 0, 1, 2 (no "every second pocket"), and there is no isolation distance under `EDEN_WINDOW`; see section 11 (e).**
- Alternative considered: one axis of far coordinates (ulp 4.9e-4 at 4096 to 8192, the earlier proposal): rejected on precision. Alternative 2: keep `EDEN_MAP_ORIGINS` and shrink the window range: not possible, the keep ring needs `[-1280, 1536)`.

### 5.5 Chunk edits (decision Q1): what survives an unload
A depleted node, a fire, a house built by a resident and a storage case are rows (`M_*` columns) that differ from the generated chunk. Unloading them without a record loses the player's world. Proposal: per-chunk deltas in `_window.deltas[(cx, cy)]` = {feature id: changed columns} for generated rows, and full row records (id, type, columns, container contents id) for runtime-created rows assigned to the chunk of their position; deltas are applied on integration and saved with the world. Smaller alternative: chunks that hold a delta are never unloaded (bounded by the row budget; the world stops growing once the budget is full of edits). Needs a decision before P5 starts.

### 5.6 Save format (decision Q2)
`EDEN_WINDOW=1` writes world format 3: schema 3, `world_format = {format: 3, spaces: [...with pocket origins...], generator_version, world_seed (string), origin, epoch}` and engine fields `_window` (origin, epoch, integrated set, deltas, allocator state, integration log tail) and `_world_seed`; geometry without masks. Refusals for 1 <-> 3 and 2 <-> 3 in the style of the two approved texts (drafts at P5 start, for approval); generator-version or seed mismatch follows the `map_guard` pattern. `compatible` in the listing already handles any pair of formats.

## 7. Risks (new or changed)
1. **Slot hazard row 1** (section 3): silent consumption from a reused slot already exists today whenever a food source row is freed and reused (burned-out forests become barrens in place, so rarely); in the window it becomes routine. Mitigated by uid + generation; the hazard remains for entries written before the change (flag-off worlds are unaffected).
2. **Re-base misses a position holder**: one-off teleport every 25 s of walking. Mitigation: the scan test; the Model-plane callback; origin-invariance trace.
3. **Contract growth**: 2.8 us per box in `world_contract`, up to 76 us per `slide` call at 484 boxes; fine for 8 residents, 7.6 ms per tick at 100. Mitigation: cached revision first, broadphase when measured on a populated window.
4. **Revision churn**: every integration changes the box set, so every consumer keyed by the contract revision (anchor-mover plans, pathing) invalidates once per integration (one chunk per tick while walking). Mitigation: stable static revision plus a separate window revision; movers replan only when their own route boxes change (**not designed**, to be settled in P5 (c)).
5. **Chunk edits** (Q1) decide how much of P5 is persistence work.
6. **Two service instances** (section 2) if the routes and the window are wired separately.
7. **Interior pockets in window mode** add a third coordinate frame to the tile index; the leak tests are the guard.
8. **Determinism of live runs** is not claimed (service timing); replay uses the log.

## 9. Note: reducing the space refresh from 158 us to the 100 us target (not done, measured)
Split measured on the golden world (149 us total in this run; 158 in the earlier one): `H_SPACE` over 100 human rows 63 us, `M_SPACE` over 1024 entity rows 87 us, `rectangles()` 0.9 us. The cost is torch call overhead (about 12 small ops per column), not arithmetic. Options, with expected effect:
1. **Skip the entity column unless the entity set changed**: guard on `(entity_count_ref[0], map_geo.geo_version, engine._force_map_refresh)`; static rows, nodes and structures change only through those; saves about 85 us in the usual tick, leaving about 65 us. Risk: a mobile entity (an animal) crossing a rectangle edge is not seen; animals cannot enter interiors (walls), so the risk is a row created by a path that does not bump the count (none known). Smallest change.
2. **Humans only on position change**: skip when `h_tensor[:, :2]` equals the previous tick's copy (one compare, about 10 us when nothing crossed); saves up to 50 us when nobody moves.
3. Compare in float32 with rectangle bounds pre-rounded to float32 (and the same rounded bounds in `space_at`): drops two `.double()` conversions, about 10 to 15 us (**inference**).
4. Tried and slower: one fused broadcast over all rectangles (194 us), a Python loop over live humans (275 us).
Option 1 alone reaches the target; it is not applied because the orchestrator asked for a note.
