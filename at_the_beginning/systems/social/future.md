# Social Future

Current limitations:
- Social depth is still narrow and focused on reproduction and grouping
- Kinship, tribe dynamics, status, alliances, and betrayal are not yet fully centralized here
- Some social consequences currently live more in memory, goals, and talk than in this module

Nuances:
- This module will likely stay a mechanics layer even if a larger relationship system grows around it
- Reproduction and group bias already create important downstream memory and planning effects

Suggested next elaborations:
- Add explicit kinship, household, group, and tribe helpers
- Add cooperation, help, escort, and ally-response mechanics
- Integrate more tightly with talk and future relationship or territory modules
- Separate high-level social reasoning from low-level social execution
