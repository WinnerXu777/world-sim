# Survival Future

Current limitations:
- Need systems are still fairly simple
- Food and water use are not yet deeply connected to inventory
- Shelter quality, rest, temperature, and comfort are still shallow

Nuances:
- Survival should remain the baseline live layer even as goals and planning get smarter
- The player incarnation is intentionally special and may bypass parts of this system

Suggested next elaborations:
- Connect eating, drinking, and carried supplies to inventory
- Add richer shelter, weather-exposure, and rest effects
- Improve resource competition and scarcity behavior
- Feed survival stress more directly into talk, escalation, and goal review
