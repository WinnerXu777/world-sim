# Perception Future

Current limitations:
- Perception is still mostly geometric and proximity-based
- Occlusion, line of sight, hearing, and uncertainty are still limited
- Human-mode sensory realism is not yet fully separated from god-view data access

Nuances:
- Many other modules depend on this one, so interface stability matters more than fancy logic here
- This is the natural place for future hearing-range and talk-audibility helpers

Suggested next elaborations:
- Add hearing, occlusion, and visibility filters
- Distinguish remembered, inferred, and directly perceived information
- Improve context objects for human-mode local-only knowledge
- Add helpers for talk, stealth, and first-person encounter design
