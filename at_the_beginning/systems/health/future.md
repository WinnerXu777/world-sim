# Health Future

Current limitations:
- Injury modeling is still coarse
- Infection and recovery are simple ticking systems
- There is little distinction yet between wounds, trauma, disease classes, and lasting disability

Nuances:
- Because health sits under survival, combat, nature, and construction, it should stay generic and deterministic
- This is a good place for shared medical state, but not for high-level social or memory logic

Suggested next elaborations:
- Add wound types, bleeding, fracture, and status effects
- Split disease depth more clearly from generic damage if infection becomes large enough
- Add recovery quality based on shelter, rest, or future medicine systems
- Expose more health summaries to talk, escalation, and reasoning payloads
