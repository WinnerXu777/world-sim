# Occupation Future

Current limitations:
- Blueprints are still routine-oriented rather than full profession execution trees
- Inventory, construction, and interaction integration is still future work
- Only a few occupations are fully task-driven so far, and their work loops are
  still narrow slices rather than full profession ecosystems

Nuances:
- Occupation should stay the role blueprint, not become a giant executor
- Sophisticated profession behavior should eventually build on tasks, planning, inventory, and interaction rather than bypass them
- Baseline occupation tools should be provisioned deterministically after survival pressure clears
- Strategic tools and weapons should come from plans and reasoning rather than from occupation defaults
- Future procurement should prefer equipped gear, inventory, and accessible storage before crafting

Suggested next elaborations:
- Connect occupations to required and optional recipes in `systems/crafting`
- Connect occupations to richer task execution and settlement economy
- Expand the medieval occupation catalog as new systems like economy, ownership, and territory appear
- Let later task/planning systems turn occupation identity into true end-to-end work loops beyond baseline tool provisioning
